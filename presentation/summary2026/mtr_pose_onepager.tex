{\LARGE\bfseries\color{navy} How Much Does Body Pose Help Pedestrian Trajectory Forecasting?}\\[2pt]
{\large A seed-replicated study, a data-forensics audit, and an agentic architecture search \hfill \normalsize Erik Sch\"utz · Sept 2026}
\vspace{2pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Answer: it doesn't --- and the null is unusually well defended.} Across 100+ multi-seed runs, three data scales (579\,$\to$\,25{,}000 scenes, 40$\times$), two training regimes, and six pose representations/integrations, \emph{correct} SMPL poses are statistically indistinguishable from \emph{scrambled} poses in every test (all hierarchical $p\geq0.37$). The decisive comparison throughout is correct pose vs.\ the same architecture fed another pedestrian's pose --- capacity and regularization cancel; only information content survives.

\begin{multicols}{2}

\section{Forensics: two bugs that reframed everything}
\textbf{Export bug (upstream pipeline).} Pose clips were grouped by scene-\emph{local} object id across all $\sim$326k scenes: nearly every training sequence carried \emph{another pedestrian's} body motion (verified bit-identical), and 4.7$\times$ of the pedestrians were silently dropped. All pre-fix results were effectively wrong-pose experiments. Fixed by per-scene re-export (299k scenes, 1.81M sequences); validated: root-heading error $41.5^\circ\!\to\!11.4^\circ$, gait--speed correlation $r\,0.26\!\to\!0.58$.

\textbf{MPJPE harness bug (ours).} Published 160.6\,mm was a frame-indexing error. Corrected chain: LiDAR-HMR \textbf{87.6\,mm} $\to$ full pipeline \textbf{92.7\,mm} root-aligned --- well below stride amplitude (300--600\,mm). RGB estimation (HMR2.0) is \emph{worse} (106.2\,mm). \textcolor{bad}{Pose quality cannot explain the null.}

LR moves minADE up to 17\%, the content signal is 0.09\% --- \emph{tuning is $\sim$190$\times$ the treatment}.

\section{New approaches}
\textbf{1. Coordinate-frame fix.} Trajectories are agent-centric but the SMPL root stayed world-frame, making body-facing-vs-travel \emph{inexpressible} (the prior frame ablation ran on corrupt data --- void). The cue is real: for standing pedestrians who then walk, facing predicts onset direction at 2.6$\times$ chance --- but only 1.3\% of agents. Frame-correct pose vs.\ its shuffled control: $-0.68\%$, $p{=}.37$. Expressible, still not convertible.

\textbf{2. Frame-invariant descriptors + early fusion (positive result).} Eight intrinsic descriptors (facing-vs-motion, turn rate, stride phase, cadence, arm swing, lean) concatenated onto the agent's input channels, \emph{no pose encoder}: \textbf{0.3711 = baseline exactly} ($-0.01\%$, $p{=}.9988$), beating the 144-d branch by \textcolor{good}{$1.66\%$ ($p{=}.0056$)}. \textbf{Pose can be carried for free --- it just doesn't pay.}

\textbf{3. Agentic search (AVO, NVIDIA 2026), running.} A headless coding agent is the variation operator over the pose-integration code: frozen commit-pinned training repo, 25k-scene proxy ($\sim$2.5\,h/eval on H100s), calibrated 0.8\% noise floor, dev/final eval split, integrity hashing. Any accepted candidate faces full confirmation (30 epochs, $n{=}5$, shuffled control, held-out split). Its hypotheses so far target temporal max-pool blindness; none accepted yet --- stagnation itself would bound what directed search can find here.

\section{Methodology as contribution}
Ten self-caught artifacts: six $n{\leq}3$ seed effects (up to $-7.6\%$, all dissolved at $n{=}5$), a last-epoch protocol artifact ($+13.4\%$, $p{=}.0002$ $\to$ n.s.), a control-revealed capacity effect, one measurement-harness bug, one tuning phantom. Paired bootstraps said $p{=}.0001$ for most; hierarchical testing + mandatory replication + wrong-pose controls killed every one. \emph{At sub-1\% effect sizes, everything is a treatment.}

\end{multicols}

{\footnotesize\textbf{Takeaway:} with provably correct, quality-verified poses and every confound tested, body pose adds nothing measurable to pedestrian minADE on Waymo. What might still change the answer: pose-to-pose interaction modelling, event-conditioned metrics (standing-onset subpopulation), or the ongoing agentic search.
