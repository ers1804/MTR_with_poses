Key Technical Fixes This Session

Fix 1 — Real Waymo Trajectories (Previous Session)
final_10fps SMPL data had observations only in past window → zero future GT → only 8 val samples had any signal. Fixed by joining with processed_scenarios_{split}/sample_{scene_id}_0.pkl files containing real 91-step Waymo trajectories. 82% val pedestrian coverage, avg 59/80 future steps valid.
Fix 2 — NaN Divergence (This Session)
At pose loss weights=1.0, training diverged to NaN at epoch 3. Two root causes:
  1. geodesic_distance_6d clamped to exactly ±1.0 → infinite gradient of acos at boundary. Fixed: use [-1+1e-7, 1-1e-7]
  2. Gradient explosion through SMPL forward pass with diverging 6D rotations. Fixed: scale pose loss weights to 0.1

Architecture

  • Base model: MTR (Motion Transformer, Shi et al. 2022)
  • Pose encoder: 2-layer GRU on past 6D SMPL pose (144D per frame) → 256D feature
  • Fusion: MLP fuses pose feature with agent trajectory feature
  • Pose decoder: Per-decoder-layer pose prediction heads (144D × 6 modes)
  • Pose losses: MPJPE + geodesic distance + GMM NLL + classification (all at weight 0.1)

Dataset Scale

  • Training: 579 scenes, 1348 pedestrians, 57 batches/epoch (batch_size=10)
  • Validation: 5171 scenes, 8282 pedestrians, 518 batches/epoch
  • Full Waymo MTR used ~486k training scenes — we are 836× smaller

Interpretation

The 3.2% improvement is modest but consistent across both minADE and minFDE (+3.1%). At this scale, both models are data-limited. The fact that pose conditioning helps at all — despite noisy SMPL estimates, sparse past observations (7 frames), and compressed pose features — is encouraging.

The convergence curves show H1_v2 has more variance early in training (pose losses add noise) but ultimately converges to a lower floor. This suggests the pose signal is providing useful inductive bias for the trajectory decoder.

Next Steps

  1. Ablate pose loss weight: Try 0.05, 0.2, 0.5 — there may be a better trade-off
  2. More training data: If the full SMPL dataset has more scenes, scale up
  3. Analyze pose prediction quality: Are the predicted poses semantically meaningful?
  4. Cross-attention pose encoding (H5): Replace GRU+MLP with cross-attention — may extract more signal from sparse past poses
